Applies to every carpet special

How a carpeted area is counted.

All three carpet specials — CARPET199, CARPET350 and CARPET431 — are quoted in areas, and an area means the same thing in all of them:

  • Each standard room counts as 1 area.
  • A large master bedroom counts as 2 areas.
  • Stairs count as 1 area.
  • A landing counts as 1 area.
  • Walk-in closets are free with service and do not count as an additional area.

For example: 3 standard bedrooms + stairs + landing = 5 areas.

Count your own home the same way before you send a request, and the figure you see on the form is the figure we start from. If the count differs on the day, we tell you before anything is cleaned.

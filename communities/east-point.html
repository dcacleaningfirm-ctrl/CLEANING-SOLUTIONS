Planning your East Point visit.

For historic homes, tell us about delicate fabrics, older floors and any access constraints. For condos or rentals, include parking and building requirements so equipment access can be planned before arrival.

How do prices, deposits and dates work?

Use the estimate form for current service options and pricing. Online booking uses a 20% deposit. A preferred date remains a request until DCA confirms availability. Scope, applicable fees and final pricing are confirmed before work begins.

Is this an HOA-sponsored event?

This is a DCA service page for individual households and local businesses. Listing a community does not mean its HOA or neighborhood association sponsors or endorses DCA.

Can I book if I’m the only neighbor interested?

Yes. You can request your own appointment. Coordinating nearby visits is optional and depends on the route.

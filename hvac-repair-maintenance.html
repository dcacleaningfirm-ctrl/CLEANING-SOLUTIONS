Book on-site HVAC troubleshooting

We know how important your family’s comfort is. We aim to get a technician to you within 2 hours of receiving your service request, subject to technician and route availability. Every service request is different, so estimated arrival times vary. We appreciate your understanding.

  • Monday through Saturday, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. Eastern: $145 deposit during regular hours.
  • After-hours emergency visits starting from 7:30 to 10:00 p.m. Eastern: $210 deposit.

The deposit covers the full on-site inspection and troubleshooting fee. If you approve the repair, DCA Cleaning Solutions credits your full deposit toward the repair cost. If you decline, the deposit covers the inspection and troubleshooting performed.

Required parts are included in the repair rate. The troubleshooting deposit covers the diagnostic visit only. After troubleshooting, DCA Cleaning Solutions will quote the repair rate, including any required parts, for your approval.

Parts lead time. DCA Cleaning Solutions keeps parts for some units in stock. Most other parts are available for same-day pickup, depending on stock and the time of day. More specialized parts may need to be ordered and may take 2 to 7 days to arrive. This is an estimate. Distributors and carriers control when parts ship and arrive.

Move-Out Cleaning

Present the property in good condition for your landlord, buyer or property manager. Deposit decisions are made by your landlord or management company, not by us.

  • Air vent & register cleaning
  • Wall spot-cleaning and scuff removal
  • Window cleaning (interior)
  • Baseboard, door frame, and trim detailing

Cleaning and refresh service, not a full property turnaround. DCA does not provide trash-outs, junk removal, hauling or repairs.     Move-in & move-out cleaning questions

    How much does move-in or move-out cleaning cost?

    DCA’s Basic Move-In / Move-Out Refresh starts at $199.99. Move-In / Move-Out Deep Clean is $549.99 and Complete Home Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning is $899.99. These are planning package prices; final scope and price are confirmed after reviewing size, condition, access and selected services.

    Which package should I choose for a move-in refresh?

    Choose Basic Move-In / Move-Out Refresh for general surface cleaning, kitchen and bathroom cleaning, floor sweeping and mopping, dusting and sanitizing treatment of compatible high-touch surfaces. Compare the higher tiers if you also need carpet, vent or appliance cleaning.

    Does the $549.99 package include furnace cleaning?

    No. Move-In / Move-Out Deep Clean includes vent-only cleaning for up to 10 vents on one HVAC system. Furnace cleaning is excluded and can be added for $60; request it when booking. The package also includes the listed Basic services, three rooms of carpet and tile cleaning, and its listed deep-cleaning tasks.

    Does Complete Home include furnace cleaning and additional systems?

    The $899.99 Complete Home Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning package includes two HVAC systems, up to 10 vents per system, with furnace cleaning included for both. Each additional HVAC system is $199.99, including furnace cleaning; select additional systems on this page to add them to your cart. Mold treatment and repairs are not included.

    Do you provide trash-outs or full apartment turnarounds?

    No. Our move cleaning is a defined cleaning and refresh service. Trash removal, hauling, repairs and full unit turnarounds are not included. Clear belongings and debris before service, and confirm any additional cleaning needs with the office.

    How should I prepare the property?

    Tell us the address, home size, move date, selected package and access arrangements. Clear the areas to be cleaned and arrange water access and safe lighting. Let us know about pets, stains and any material or product sensitivities before your appointment.

    Can I book cleaning before handing over the keys?

    Call (470) 485-3123 with your move date or select a package online. Appointments depend on route availability. Allow time for inspection, cleaning and drying before furniture arrives or keys are returned. Cleaning does not guarantee a landlord’s security-deposit decision.

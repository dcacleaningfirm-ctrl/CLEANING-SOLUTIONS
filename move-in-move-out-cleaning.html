Professional Move In / Move Out Cleaning

Leave your old place clean or start fresh in your new one. Our packages cover carpet extraction, air duct cleaning, appliance cleaning and sanitizing treatment of compatible surfaces.

View Cleaning Packages

What's Included

Whether you are moving into a new home or preparing to hand over the keys, each package has a defined scope. Package contents are confirmed with you before work begins, and anything outside the listed scope is quoted separately rather than assumed.

Move-In Cleaning

Start in a home that has been cleaned throughout before you unpack.

  • Kitchen & bathroom cleaning
  • Cabinet interior wipe-down
  • Appliance surface cleaning (oven, fridge, dishwasher)
  • Carpet hot-water extraction
  • Air duct cleaning — removes accessible accumulated debris in the service scope
  • Floor sweeping and mopping
  • Light fixture & ceiling fan dusting
  • Window sill and baseboard wipe-down
  • Closet and shelf interior cleaning
  • Sanitizing treatment applied to compatible surfaces according to the manufacturer's label. Product information is available upon request.

Move-Out Cleaning

Present the property in good condition for your landlord, buyer or property manager. Deposit decisions are made by your landlord or management company, not by us.

  • Deep kitchen degreasing (stovetop, hood, backsplash)
  • Bathroom tile scrubbing & grout cleaning
  • Appliance interior cleaning (oven, microwave, fridge)
  • Carpet cleaning with spot and odor treatment where needed
  • Air vent & register cleaning
  • Wall spot-cleaning and scuff removal
  • Window cleaning (interior)
  • Baseboard, door frame, and trim detailing
  • Garage/patio sweep (if applicable)
  • Sanitizing treatment applied to compatible surfaces according to label directions

What these packages cover. Cleaning is not repair, restoration or remediation. Permanent stains, dye loss, worn or damaged flooring, existing paint and drywall damage, hard-water etching and mineral scale may not improve. Sanitizing treatment is applied to compatible surfaces per the manufacturer's EPA-registered label directions and we make no claim beyond the label; it is not mold remediation, and moisture sources must be identified and corrected. Air duct cleaning, where included, removes the accessible accumulated debris in the service scope. We stand behind the quality of our workmanship and will address concerns related to the agreed scope of service. Please tell us about children, pets, allergies or sensitivities before service so we can select suitable products.

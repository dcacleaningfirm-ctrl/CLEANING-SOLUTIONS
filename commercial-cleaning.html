Commercial carpet cleaning

Truck-mounted extraction for high-traffic commercial carpet.

DCA uses professional truck-mounted hot-water extraction for carpet in offices, corridors, common areas, meeting rooms, leasing offices, worship spaces, restaurants and other commercial environments. We review traffic patterns, access, soil load and scheduling needs before the work begins.

  • Routine maintenance and restorative carpet cleaning
  • High-traffic lanes, entrances, hallways and common areas
  • Spot treatment and fiber-appropriate pre-treatment
  • Pet, smoke and organic odor treatment options where appropriate
  • After-hours or coordinated service windows when available
  • Single-site and multi-property scheduling

Commercial air duct cleaning

Air-system cleaning coordinated around building operations.

For commercial and managed properties, DCA evaluates the number of HVAC systems, supply registers, returns, access points and equipment condition before finalizing scope. We can clean supply and return pathways and inspect accessible air-handler components so property teams understand what is present before add-on treatment is recommended.

  • Supply and return register cleaning
  • HVAC-system and accessible air-handler inspection
  • Multi-unit and multi-system properties
  • Apartment turnovers and common-area systems
  • Optional compatible-surface treatment when separately approved
  • Scope documented before work begins

A simple commercial process

Scope first. Quote second. Work third.

  1. Request a quote. Send the property details, floor plan or approximate scope.
  2. Walkthrough when needed. Larger commercial scopes can be reviewed before pricing is finalized.
  3. Written scope. We confirm what is included, access requirements, schedule and pricing before work begins.
  4. Service and documentation. Work is completed to the approved scope, with invoicing arranged for the approved account terms.

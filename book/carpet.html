Areas

How many carpeted areas?

Set this to zero and continue if you do not want carpet cleaning on this visit.

An area is a single enclosed carpeted room up to roughly 300 square feet. A large master bedroom counts as 2 areas, stairs count as 1, a landing counts as 1, and walk-in closets are free with service. Larger open-plan spaces are measured on site and counted as more than one area; we tell you that before we start, not after.

Cleaning several areas at once? The whole-home carpet specials cover 5, 10 or 12 areas for one price — see the promotions page to book one instead.

The method

Hot-water extraction, in a diagram.

The equipment stays in the vehicle. Heated cleaning solution goes in under pressure through a hose, and the same pass recovers the water and the soil it has loosened.

Cross-section

What the pass reaches.

  • Furniture that two people can safely move is moved and replaced; heavy or fragile pieces stay where they are and we clean around them.
  • Spots are pre-treated by type where we can identify them. Some marks are permanent changes to the fiber — dye loss, bleach, burns, wear patterns — and cleaning will not reverse those.
  • Drying usually takes a few hours with airflow. We tell you what to expect for your fiber and conditions on the day.

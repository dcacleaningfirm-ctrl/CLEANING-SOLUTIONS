The method

How we decide how to clean your fabric.

Cross-section

Woven fabric, in section.

  • We read the manufacturer's label code where one survives, and identify the fiber by hand and by construction where it does not.
  • Colorfast testing happens in an inconspicuous area before any product reaches a visible panel.
  • Dye migration, pile crush on velvet, shrinkage on loose covers and existing wear are risks that belong to the fabric, not to the method. Where we see one, you hear about it before we begin.
